INVESTOR ELIGIBILITY & RESTRICTIONS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

This offering is restricted exclusively to Professional Investors (PI) as defined
under Schedule 1 of the Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO s.1 Sch 1),
including institutional investors, licensed corporations, and individuals with
a portfolio of not less than HKD 8,000,000.

Accredited and institutional investors from approved jurisdictions may participate
subject to applicable local law requirements.

Transfer restrictions are enforced on-chain via the ERC-3643 compliance module.
Only whitelisted, eligible investors who have completed identity verification may
hold or receive HCRT tokens. On-chain enforcement ensures no transfer can occur
to non-compliant addresses.

RISK FACTORS & DISCLOSURE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Investors should carefully consider the following risk factors before investing:

1. Market Risk: Property valuations may fluctuate based on Singapore real estate
   market conditions, interest rate changes, and macroeconomic factors.

2. Liquidity Risk: Secondary market trading of HCRT tokens is not guaranteed.
   Redemption is available annually during designated windows.

3. Regulatory Risk: Changes in SFC tokenisation regulations or REIT Code requirements
   may affect the token structure and transferability.

4. Technology Risk: Smart contract vulnerabilities, network outages, or blockchain
   forks may impact token operations. The smart contract code has been fully audited
   by an independent third-party security firm (Certik, audit report #HCK-2026-042).

5. Concentration Risk: The portfolio is geographically concentrated in Asia-Pacific
   Central district.

Asset Valuation Methodology: NAV is calculated semi-annually using discounted cash
flow (DCF) and capitalisation rate methods by an independent RICS valuer.

Redemption Terms: Annual redemption window (Q4 each year), subject to 90-day notice.
Redemption price is based on prevailing NAV minus a 1.5% redemption fee.

Fee Structure:
  — Management Fee: 1.0% p.a. of NAV
  — Custodian Fee: 0.15% p.a. of NAV
  — Performance Fee: 15% of returns above 6% p.a. hurdle rate

Conflict of Interest: Harbour Capital Asset Management Limited may provide services
to affiliated entities. All related-party transactions are disclosed in the annual
report and approved by the independent board committee.

════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
TECHNOLOGY & SMART CONTRACT
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The HCRT token is deployed as a smart contract on Mantle Network using the
ERC-3643 T-REX standard. The smart contract code has been subject to a comprehensive
independent security audit to identify any vulnerability prior to mainnet deployment.

On-chain compliance is enforced through a ComplianceOracle contract that gates
token minting and transfers based on AI-verified compliance scores and KYC status.
The whitelist of eligible investors is maintained in an on-chain IdentityRegistry.
