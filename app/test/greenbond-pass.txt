RISK FACTORS & DISCLOSURE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Key risk factors investors must consider:

1. Credit Risk: Issuer default risk. Mitigated by investment-grade credit rating (Moody's A3).
2. Interest Rate Risk: Bond valuation is sensitive to market interest rate movements.
3. Regulatory Risk: Changes to SFC regulations on tokenised securities may affect
   the bond's legal structure and transferability.
4. Technology Risk: Smart contract security vulnerabilities. The smart contract
   has been independently audited by PeckShield with zero critical findings.
5. Liquidity Risk: No guaranteed secondary market. Redemption at maturity only.
6. ESG Risk: Failure of green projects to achieve certification targets may affect
   ESG ratings and investor demand.

Valuation: The bond is valued at par (face value) plus accrued interest.
Mark-to-market valuation is available via Bloomberg terminal feed.

Redemption: Full principal redemption at maturity (15 May 2031).
Optional early redemption at issuer's discretion with 30-day notice and
a make-whole premium.

Fees:
  — Issuance fee: 0.5% of total proceeds
  — Annual administration fee: 0.10% of outstanding balance
  — Early redemption fee: Make-whole + 0.25%

Conflict of Interest: Harbour Sustainable Finance Corporation and its affiliates
may hold positions in the underlying green projects. All conflicts are disclosed
in the prospectus supplement.
