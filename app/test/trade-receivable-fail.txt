This document describes a trade finance tokenisation opportunity for internal
review purposes only.

OVERVIEW
We are looking to tokenise a batch of trade receivables from Pearl River
Manufacturing Company. The company exports electronics components to buyers
in Europe and North America.

Invoice Portfolio Summary:
- Total face value: USD 3,200,000
- Number of invoices: 47
- Average invoice tenor: 90 days
- Expected yield: 8-12% annualised

The buyers have good credit history with the exporter over the past 3 years.
Most buyers are Fortune 500 companies.

PROPOSED STRUCTURE
We plan to issue digital tokens on a blockchain. Each token will be worth
about USD 1,000. Investors can buy these tokens and receive payments when
the invoices are paid.

The tokens will be listed on our platform for secondary trading after the
initial offering.

RETURNS
Investors should expect returns of approximately 8-10% per annum based on
historical invoice payment rates. There is some risk of buyer default but
this has been minimal historically.
