UNDERLYING ASSET
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Asset Type: Tokenised Trade Receivables (Supply Chain Finance)
Underlying Asset: A diversified pool of verified trade receivables from
  Asia-Pacific exporters supplying to investment-grade buyers globally.

Portfolio Composition:
  — 120 invoices across 15 anchor buyers (minimum BBB+ credit rating)
  — Total face value: USD 12,000,000
  — Weighted average tenor: 75 days
  — Geographic diversification: EU (40%), US (35%), Japan (25%)

Each token represents a fractional legal claim on the pool of receivables.
Legal title and ownership of each invoice has been assigned to the SPV trustee
via a deed of assignment registered in Singapore. The assignment creates a
perfected legal claim enforceable against the buyers.

RISK FACTORS & DISCLOSURE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Risk Factors:
1. Buyer Default Risk: Non-payment by invoice buyers. Mitigated by buyer
   credit insurance (Euler Hermes AAA-rated policy).
2. Dilution Risk: New invoice tranches may dilute existing token holders.
3. Valuation Risk: Invoice valuation based on face value minus discount rate.
   Independent monthly valuation report provided by Deloitte.
4. Regulatory Risk: SFC regulatory changes may affect tokenised security structure.
5. Liquidity Risk: No guaranteed secondary market for HSCFT tokens.
6. Technology Risk: Smart contract vulnerability. Independent audit completed
   by Trail of Bits (report #TOB-HBR-2026). No critical security issue identified.
7. FX Risk: USD-denominated invoices from non-USD buyers carry currency exposure.

Valuation Methodology: Each invoice is valued at face value discounted at the
agreed financing rate. NAV is updated weekly by the independent administrator.

Redemption: Tokens are redeemed as invoices are paid by buyers, typically
within 30–90 days of issuance. Early redemption subject to 2% fee.

Fees:
  — Origination fee: 0.75% of portfolio face value
  — Administration fee: 0.20% p.a. of outstanding balance
  — Performance fee: 10% of returns above 7% hurdle

Conflict of Interest: Harbour Trade Finance SPV may engage affiliated entities
for invoice origination. All related-party arrangements are disclosed in this
prospectus and subject to independent trustee oversight.
